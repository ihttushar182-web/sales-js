02 / DIAGNOSIS

The four leaks that cap
expert-led businesses.

Named after what I keep finding in audits of training, consulting and service brands.
L1LEAK 01

Believed too late

The page opens with a biography. Buyers need an outcome in the first 4 seconds; your CV is their third question, not your first sentence.

L2LEAK 02

One offer, flat price

A single “hire me” makes every conversation a yes/no on the biggest number you have. A 3-rung ladder turns no into “not yet this size”.

L3LEAK 03

Silent follow-up

Most enquiries die between reply 1 and the call. No recap, no case, no deadline — so the loudest vendor wins by default.

L4LEAK 04

Traffic with nowhere to land

DMs go to a Facebook page, posts go to a profile, calls go to nothing. A one-way page per message is the cheapest lift in existence.

Find your leak in 5 minutes → You get the score, the maths and the fix list whether or not you hire me.

Big following, small pipeline.

Tens of thousands of followers, ~30 years of credibility, and a booking process that ran on voice notes and WhatsApp back-and-forth. I rebuilt the offer as a ladder (diagnostic workshop → quarterly programme → annual leadership retainer), put a 5-minute qualification funnel in front of every post, and installed a 7-touch follow-up spine with a 24-hour recap and a 7-day case send.

“I have no time to give a consultant.”+
The whole engagement is about 3 hours of you: two 45-minute interviews and a 30-minute review. Everything else I take from what you already have — DMs, calls you recorded, old decks, your best clients' words. I write; you approve.
“How will I know it worked?”+
A baseline is recorded on day 1, before anything changes. Then four numbers: visitor→lead, lead→call, call→paid, average value per closed deal. Fortnightly, one page. If a metric is not instrumented, it does not go in the report and it does not count as a claim.
“Do you work with individuals, or only companies?”+
Individuals are often the better fit — a trainer, consultant, doctor, coach or founder with real authority and no way to be bought. One person, one offer, one page, one follow-up spine is exactly the shape I like. Two such clients per quarter, so reply speed matters.
